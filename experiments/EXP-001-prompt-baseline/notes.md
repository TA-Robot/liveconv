# EXP-001 working notes

Do not collect final results until the experiment status is `approved` and every
`TBD` that can affect comparison is resolved.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Smoke fixture manifest is versioned and frozen.
- [ ] Control and variant prompts are immutable.
- [ ] Current model and voice revisions are recorded.
- [ ] Browser, OS, audio device, and DaaS route are recorded.
- [ ] Warmup, repetitions, randomization, and sample count are fixed.
- [ ] Rating rubric and rater authorization are fixed.
- [ ] Decision thresholds are numeric.
- [ ] Artifact location, access, and retention are approved.

## Observations

Record protocol questions here during design. Once execution begins, append
timestamped deviations and invalidate the run when the frozen plan requires it.
